/home/ssd_disk/LA_export (moved from /home/drod/LA_export): Lv04AnglersTunnel, Lv07EagleTower, Field (overworld, 585 screens as Field_<row><col>.gltf) with glTF models, textures, actors, render/level.json (per-room spot lights, SH, cubemaps, roofs/fades). godot_viewer/ (owned by another session, don't edit) rebuilds rooms with the game's lighting; as of 2026-10-13 Daniel says it's fairly complete and calibrated against 3 game captures (calib_3rooms_game_vs_godot.png, README table).

Measured facts: room 15×12 units, wall top / roof plane y=3.12, Link ~1.45 tall (same scale as Kai). Viewer calibrated: camera pitch 65°, vertical FOV 28°, distance 23, follows Link in X at 60%; linear tonemap; since 2026-10-13 auto exposure 0.325·L^-0.55, soft toe (x<0.3 → power 1.2), saturation 1.0 (the old fixed 0.88 + toe 0.28/2.23 + sat 0.8 was fitted on Lv04 only). Daniel chose an in-between for Epiphany's LA test (toe power 1.7, sat 0.9, ambient 1.3) for contrast; billboard HDR flame + flickering omni (3.5, range 4) for sconces; side-only vignette, glow additive threshold 1.2, per-room SH ambient (cool), spot lights pointing down to room centre, actors NOT leaned, neighbour rooms visible (no black lids). Water recipe in la_shaders/la_water.gdshader (ported procedurally to src/world/water/water_la.gdshader). Non-current rooms hidden by flat black DungeonRoof lids at y=3.12; DungeonFade = flat vertex-alpha plane, black at the room edge → 0 within 0.75 u inward. Env: black background, filmic, glow softlight 0.6, SSAO r0.8 i1.5, tilt-shift DOF (near/far ±3 m from focus).

Update 2026-10-13 (RenderDoc on Eden, Lv07 08B + Lv04): the game has NO auto exposure and NO vignette pass. Tonemap = Hejl/Burgess-Dawson: x=max(0,hdr·2.0−0.004), y=x(6.2x+0.5)/(x(6.2x+1.7)+0.06), out=y^2.2, exposure 2.0 in every level (viewer/la_tonemap.gd, CompositorEffect POST_TRANSPARENT, Godot tonemap LINEAR exp 1). The apparent vignette is black fade geometry at room edges plus unlit edges. SSAO multiplies all light. Bloom threshold 0.2 (Lv07) / 0.1 (Lv04) before exposure, tinted (0.753,0.847,0.733). Ambient = per-room SH × level scale (Lv04 0.45, Lv07 0.6) × hemisphere tint mix(ground, sky, n·up) with k (la_sh.gdshaderinc). Lv08 Turtle Rock added (lava = water shader + emissive bed + glow cards, uncalibrated). The auto-exposure/toe model above is now superseded. Camera read from the game's view cbuffer: eye = room centre + (0, 21.545, 9.52), pitch 65°, vFOV 28.12°, looks 0.53 m north of the room centre, FIXED per room (no follow; a 15 m room fits in ~21 m of view). Ours (Chullpa, 2026-10-13): pitch 65, fov 28, 34 m, same DOF margins, but rooms are 26×14 so the camera pans in X. Water (Daniel, 2026-10-13: "ahora está perfecto el agua"): la_water.gdshader is the game's colour model transcribed (tint by depth × refraction + caustics, PBR metal 0.5, × blend_color (0,0.8,1), ALPHA = t/0.5); ported to src/world/water/water_la.gdshader with our noise textures and a flat ambient_light instead of la_sh_eval. Viewer finished 2026-10-13 (final water re-ported in 201b7dac: caustics projected on the floor in metres, flat reflection_color instead of the room cube, depth write). Later RenderDoc found the game DOES have a vignette: a full-screen black quad multiplying HDR before bloom, radial 1 until d=0.5, 0.74 at 0.85, 0.24 at 1.4 (table in viewer/la_tonemap.gd). Game SSAO multiplies the whole HDR scene (viewer/la_ssao.gd); Godot's SSAO only darkens ambient. Our dungeons still use Godot tonemap/SSAO, no vignette (Daniel removed ours earlier).
