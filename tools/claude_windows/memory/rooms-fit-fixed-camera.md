Daniel (2026-10-14): every dungeon room is 14 tall and at least 22 wide, like the Chullpa and the Queñoa. Totora, Cumbre and the temple were built at 14×10 / 14×12 and get redesigned (cards 11.178–11.180). This replaces his 2026-10-13 idea of ~15×12 rooms that fit one fixed LA camera.

Same day: no cracked walls in D1 (11.177); LA wall tops show the wall tile color instead of the black veil, a blurred screen-edge vignette hides what's beyond, and DOF only blurs what's very high or close to the camera (12.32).

Update (2026-10-15, his note on 11.179): with the configurable vignette and camera the 14×≥22 rule is no longer needed; rooms may have different sizes (like the temple's final boss room), but each room must be centerable on camera and hide the other rooms.

**Why:** Daniel's direction after seeing the small temple rooms; the 14×10 rooms looked wrong on screen.
**How to apply:** any room size is fine; check each room centers on camera and the others stay hidden. Related: [[la-walls-baked-levels]], [[dungeon-chain]].
